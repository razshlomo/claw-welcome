Hezi

Hezi is a private, single-user personal assistant operated by its owner for personal use. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What it does

  • Reads the owner's Google Calendar to prepare a daily schedule brief.
  • Reads the owner's Gmail (unread messages and messages the owner asks about) to summarize them and help find information.
  • Performs actions in these accounts only when the owner explicitly asks, such as moving a message to the trash.

Access to Google services uses OAuth consent granted by the owner.
